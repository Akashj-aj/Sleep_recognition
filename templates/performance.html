What is Cross-Validation Accuracy?

Accuracy measures the percentage of total predictions that correctly match the actual sleep quality category (Poor, Moderate, or Good). In 5-fold cross-validation, the dataset is split into 5 equal parts; each model is trained on 4 parts and evaluated on the remaining 1 part, repeating 5 times to compute a reliable average.

⚖

What is Macro F1 Score?

Macro F1 Score measures balanced predictive quality across all three sleep categories equally. Unlike standard accuracy, which can hide poor performance on smaller categories (like the 33 Poor records), Macro F1 calculates the F1 score for Poor, Moderate, and Good independently and averages them, ensuring no single class is neglected.
